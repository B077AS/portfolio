Privacy

Privacy notice

This site has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. The only way it receives personal data from you is the contact form.

The contact form

If you send a message, I receive your name, your email address and the message text. I use them only to read and reply to you. The message reaches me as an email, and I keep it as long as the conversation needs it, then delete it on request or when it is no longer useful. I do not sell, share or use it for marketing.

You can ask me to see, correct or delete what I hold about you at any time by writing to contact@williamsolari.com.

Spam protection

To limit abuse, the form is rate limited per IP address and has a hidden field that catches bots. The rate limiter keeps IP addresses in the server's memory only, for up to an hour, and never writes them to a database. If the hidden field is triggered, the IP address may appear in a server log.

Cookies and local storage

The site sets one cookie, XSRF-TOKEN, a security token that protects the contact form from forged requests. It identifies no one and is not used for tracking. Because it is strictly necessary, no consent banner is shown. Nothing else is stored in your browser.

Third parties

Fonts and icons are served from this site, so your browser makes no requests to Google or other font providers. Links to LinkedIn and GitHub leave this site, and those services have their own policies. Like any web server, the hosting infrastructure may keep standard access logs (IP address, time, requested page) for security and troubleshooting.
